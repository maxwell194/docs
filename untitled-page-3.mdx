## **Security Best Practices**

- Rotate API keys every 30 days for enhanced security.
- Avoid sharing your API key with third parties unless necessary, and sign formal agreements (DSA or DPA) to ensure data compliance.
- Use a proxy or wrapper when integrating third-party services, exposing only the needed data—not your raw API key.
- Restrict API usage with IP whitelisting.
- Monitor API key usage and immediately reset keys if suspicious activity is detected or partnerships end.

## Error Message Guide

| **Status Code** | **Common Cause** | **Resolution** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `401 Unauthorized` | Invalid or missing API key. | Verify that your raw API key is provided in the `Authorization` header without any "Bearer" prefix. |
| `403 Forbidden` | Missing `x-oid` header or insufficient key permissions. | Ensure the `x-oid` header contains your correct Agency OID. Verify that the requested creator or employee is explicitly included in your API key's permission scope. |
| `429 Too Many Requests` | Agency rate limit exceeded. | You have exceeded the 1,000 QPM agency threshold across your API keys. Pause or space out requests before retrying. |
| `500 / 503 Internal Error` | Temporary API or database error. | Retry the request after a short interval. If the issue persists, contact support and provide the `x-request-id` from the response headers. |

## Common use cases

### **Financial operations & reporting**

- **Automated reporting:** Sync creator revenue and marketing data directly to client-facing or internal dashboards.
- **Automated invoicing & workflows:** Automate bi-weekly or monthly invoice calculations using raw earnings data, including syncing OnlyFans net income data directly into your own systems.
- **Payroll & commission calculator:** A back-office tool for agency operations that automatically calculates employee earnings based on custom commission percentages and their individual sales.
- **Target Tracking:** View real-time data on agency standing and compare current revenue breakdowns against set goals by individual creator.

### **Team performance & analytics**

- **Employee sales performance:** Track individual chatter sales data breakdowns, verify sales, and compare results against set sales targets.
- **Sales leaderboard & gamification:** A highly visual dashboard that ranks employees by sales performance to foster friendly internal competition and drive higher revenue.
- **Performance & efficiency dashboard:** A dedicated space focused entirely on evaluating the individual or team performance of the employees running the accounts.
- **PPV & sales funnel analytics:** Analyze the sales funnel of the team, specifically measuring how effectively they are selling high-value content (PPVs).

### **Marketing & campaign optimization**

- **Campaign optimization:** Feed marketing link stats into your own tracking tools to identify which campaigns drive the highest ROI.
- **SEO & collaboration tracking:** Connect the API to a custom-built app that tracks earnings from specific tracking links, allowing you to calculate and sync payouts.
- **Link performance & revenue attribution:** Use custom-built apps via the API to track subscriptions and fan spending associated with specific promotional links.
- **Profile visibility:** Monitor a creator’s visibility, reach, and popularity trends across the platform.

### **Fan management & retention**

- **Fan growth & retention:** Track subscriber health, predict revenue stability, and analyze fan behavior and lifetime value over time.
- **Fan engagement & interaction:** Analyze how active and effective creators (or their teams) are in communicating and engaging with fans to build loyalty.

### **Risk management**

- **Chargeback & risk mitigation:** Protect your revenue with an automated notification system for chargebacks and risk management directed towards the chatters.
