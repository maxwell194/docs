---
title: "Data retention"
---

Retention windows and data availability thresholds vary by endpoint:

| **Category** | **Endpoint / Scope** | **Retention Window** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Creator information** | General profile data & connected creators | Full historical data |
| **Creator status log** | Full history of creator status modifications | From June 1, 2026 onwards |
| **Revenue breakdown** | Detailed earnings, transaction details, & refund details | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
| **Transaction performance** | Sales attribution details with employee breakdowns (supports all creators, including deleted/unlinked) | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
| **Reassigned sales log** | Manual assignment records for sales data | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
| **Marketing analytics** | Tracking links | Full historical data |
| **Marketing analytics** | Free trial links | Full historical data |
| **Marketing analytics** | Profile promotions | Last 10,000 campaigns from up to 365 days ago |
| **Marketing analytics** | Link fans | Full historical data |
| **Employee data** | Employee lists & employee-to-creator assignments | Full historical data |
| **Employee reports** | Sales performance & chat activity breakdowns by employee | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
| **Creator reports** | Daily performance metrics (platform ranking, fan status, growth, renewals, chat activity, visitors) | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
| **Message analytics** | Records of automated & priority mass messages | Full historical data |
| **Invoice** | Monthly Infloww billing and invoice summaries | Maximum of last 1 year of historical data |
